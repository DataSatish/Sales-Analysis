# 📈 Sales Analysis — Reviving Stagnant Revenue

## 📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes **2 years of sales data** for a company experiencing flat revenue growth. The goal was to identify the root causes of stagnation, uncover hidden opportunities, and deliver clear recommendations to management through KPI dashboards and trend analysis.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem
The company had seen no meaningful sales growth for 2 consecutive years. Leadership needed answers to:
- Which regions, products, or sectors are underperforming?
- Are there any growth opportunities being missed?
- What specific actions can reverse the stagnation trend?

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
| Tool | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| Power Query | Data cleaning & transformation |
| Power BI | KPI dashboard & visualization |

---

## 🔄 Project Workflow
1. **Data Cleaning** — Cleaned and transformed raw sales data using Power Query (handled nulls, standardized formats, removed inconsistencies)
2. **KPI Development** — Defined and calculated key performance indicators for sales performance tracking
3. **Multi-dimensional Analysis** — Analyzed performance across regions, product categories, and business sectors
4. **Dashboard Creation** — Built interactive Power BI dashboards to visualize trends and comparisons
5. **Recommendation Report** — Identified key growth opportunities and delivered a structured recommendation report to management

---

## 📊 Key Findings
- Pinpointed specific regions consistently underperforming relative to targets
- Identified product categories with declining demand versus growing demand
- Sector-level analysis revealed untapped market opportunities
- Delivered prioritized action plan to management to break the revenue plateau
